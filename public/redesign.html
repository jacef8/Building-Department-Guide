What are the setbacks on this parcel, and can they build near the well?

Setbacks are 20 feet on every side

On Residential (Low Density) this parcel has a 20 ft front, 20 ft side and 20 ft rear setback Building Guide, p. 1. On a square one-acre lot that leaves a buildable area of about 28,500 sq ft — roughly two thirds of the lot.

The well is the harder half of the question. The Land Development Code puts a 200-foot zone of exclusion around a potable water supply well, but writes in "(but not individual private wells)" Land Development Code, p. 112. A 200-foot radius covers 2.9 acres — almost three times this whole parcel — so on a lot this size the rule either does nothing or it stops everything, with no middle ground.

Say this carefully at the counter

The Comprehensive Plan's Policy 1-5 states the same 200-foot exclusion without the private-well carve-out, and where the two disagree the Plan controls. Read literally it would stop an owner building within 200 feet of their own well. That is on the county's list of amendments to make. Until it is fixed, apply the Code — private wells are not subject to the exclusion — and tell the applicant the Plan language is being corrected.
